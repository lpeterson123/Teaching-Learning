About this dashboard

This is a working companion to a longer faculty document. It surfaces the practice moves and reflective questions that anchor each phase of the year — visit early in the summer, again midway through the semester, and once more before grades close.

Two ways to read

Browse by Phase when you're working inside a moment in the year — summer planning, mid-semester recalibration, end-of-term reflection. Browse by Focus Area when you want to see how one thread — cultural competency, teaching & learning, AI, or flex blocks — evolves across the whole year.
